Story

This tale unfolds on a planet governed by demons.

Residing in Apt. 1501-1502 of Coincident Apartment are four demons.
Private detective John, as ever, was occupied with his usual fare of cases, like tracking down lost cats.
Then, one day, Harvey—the tenant living just across the hall—approached John, pleading for assistance in investigating a mysterious Research Institute.
Thus, an utterly extraordinary daily life began to unfold.

Lineage Abilities

It is generally believed that Lineage Abilities originated as a blessing bestowed by the Demon God, passed down through generations.
Alternatively, Lineage Abilities can also emerge when an incarnation of the Demon God descends to the mortal realm and procreates with a demon, resulting in offspring possessing such powers.
